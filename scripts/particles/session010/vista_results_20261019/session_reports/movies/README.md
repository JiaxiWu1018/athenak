All run at 12 fps, 2 Mref per playback second, with 1,333 encoded frames
(about 111.1s). Actual source states are held between saved times; motion is
not interpolated. Field movies use 445 snapshots and the particle movie 112.
Every final saved state is included. Source times and encoding metadata:
[MOVIES.json](/data/jiaxiwu/NRPIC/GI_in_cluster/session_010_vista_preparation_20261013/evidence/vista_reconciliation_20261019/vista_analysis/MOVIES.json).

The 3D cohort is fixed at t=0: 40,000 envelope and 25,000 particles per clump.
Removed tags are never replenished; the movie is not the full five-million
population. Disappearing sampled particles do not imply a disappearing black
hole. White energy pixels mean nonpositive sampled E. Scales are fixed and
native leaf slices are resampled at pixel centers without smoothing.
